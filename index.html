Confused? We've Got the Answers!

Why should we choose you?+

I bring a cinematic, story-first approach to every project — whether it's a wedding film, commercial edit, or brand reel. My focus is on authentic emotion, clean pacing, and visuals that feel premium and personal.

How is your work different from others?+

Every film is edited with narrative intent, not just pretty shots. I balance aesthetics with messaging for commercial work, and build emotional arcs for weddings and events that feel cinematic rather than generic.

Do you travel for projects?+

Yes, I travel across India for the right projects. Travel and accommodation are discussed transparently before confirmation and are additional to the base quote.

How are your services priced?+

Pricing depends on project scope, deliverables, and timeline. I share a clear quote after understanding your needs — no hidden costs, and packages can be tailored for weddings, commercials, or ongoing content.

What are your delivery timelines?+

Commercial projects are typically 1–2 weeks. Wedding films take 3–4 weeks after the event. Reels and short-form edits are delivered within 5–7 working days.

What formats do you deliver?+

All final videos are delivered in Full HD (1080p) or 4K via Google Drive. Instagram-optimised versions are included at no extra charge.

Know more

Darshan
Agrawal

Filmmaker · Video Editor · Storyteller

Stories have always mattered more to me than just visuals.

What started with editing slowly became a way of understanding moments — finding emotion in real reactions, building rhythm through pacing and turning raw footage into something people can feel.

Today, I work across wedding films and commercial projects, shaping footage into cinematic stories with intention, emotion and a refined visual approach.

Beyond personal projects, I collaborate with wedding filmmakers, agencies and creative teams — supporting them through editing and post-production while helping transform timelines into polished films.

Whether it is a wedding celebration or a commercial campaign, every project begins the same way:

Understand the story first. Build the edit around it.

Because great films are not built from footage alone — they are built from the moments inside it.
