What draws you to editing?

I'm fascinated by how pacing, sound, and visuals can shape emotions and keep people engaged. Every project is an opportunity to tell a story more effectively.

I study high-performing edits, recreate references, experiment constantly, and learn through feedback and iteration.

THE PROCESS

Every frame is built on a foundation of craft — four pillars that define how we turn raw footage into cinematic stories.

01
Colour Grading

Mood is
Everything.

We don't just correct colour — we sculpt it. Every grade starts with the story's emotional core: warm and intimate for hospitality films, clean and sharp for corporate edits, rich and textured for fashion campaigns. Using DaVinci Resolve and custom LUTs, we build a palette unique to your brand.

  • Custom LUT development
  • Scene-by-scene grading
  • Brand-consistent colour palette
02
Music Design

Sound that
Moves People.

The right score turns a good film into an unforgettable one. We curate, compose, and layer music that breathes with your visuals — building tension, releasing emotion, and anchoring your brand's voice in every note. From licensed tracks to original compositions, audio is never an afterthought.

  • Licensed & original scoring
  • Sound design & foley
  • Mix & master for web & broadcast

We deliver in full 4K — optimised for every platform from cinema screens to Instagram reels. Our render pipeline handles complex compositing, motion graphics, and VFX without sacrificing a single frame of quality. You get files ready for broadcast, social, and digital OOH in one delivery.

  • Multi-format delivery
  • Platform-optimised compression

Why should we choose you?

I bring a cinematic, story-first approach to every project — whether it's a commercial edit, or brand reel. My focus is on authentic emotion, clean pacing, and visuals that feel premium and personal.

How is your work different from others?

Every film is edited with narrative intent, not just pretty shots. I balance aesthetics with messaging for commercial work, and build emotional arcs for weddings and events that feel cinematic rather than generic.

Do you travel for projects?

STORIES WERE NEVER THE REASON I STARTED.

Curiosity was.

Curiosity about why some edits make you stay. Why certain moments feel louder in silence. Why a cut at the right frame can change everything.

Over time, editing stopped feeling like software and started feeling like observation.

I spend a lot of time studying things most people scroll past — the rhythm of a conversation, the weight of a pause, the way sound changes emotion, the reason audiences stop and watch.

Today, I work across commercial projects and short-form content, constantly rebuilding references, experimenting with ideas, and learning through iteration.

I don't chase transitions.

I chase feeling.

Because the best edits aren't remembered for how they moved

They're remembered for how they made people feel.
